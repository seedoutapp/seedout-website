Frequently Asked Questions

What does SeedOut flag?

SeedOut flags harmful ingredients in two tiers — red (Avoid) and yellow (Use Caution). Red flags include seed oils like canola, sunflower, and soybean oil, artificial colors, artificial sweeteners, high fructose corn syrup, carrageenan, maltodextrin, and more. Yellow flags include natural flavors, yeast extract, soy lecithin, enriched flours, and other ingredients worth being aware of.

Is SeedOut free?

Yes — SeedOut is free to download and use. Barcode scanning and ingredient analysis are always free.

A product I scanned came back as unknown. What do I do?

If a product isn't found in our database, try scanning again with better lighting and ensure the barcode is clearly visible. If it still doesn't work, please email us with the product name and barcode number and we'll add it to our database.

Where does SeedOut get its ingredient data?

SeedOut uses the Open Food Facts database, one of the largest open food product databases in the world, combined with our own curated ingredient flag list built from nutritional research.

How do I report a bug or incorrect scan result?

Please email us at support@seedoutapp.com with the product name, barcode number, and a description of what you think is incorrect. We take data accuracy very seriously.

Is my data private?

Yes. SeedOut does not collect or store personal data. You can read our full privacy policy here.
